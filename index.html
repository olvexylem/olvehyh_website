Olivia Huynh (b.2004, Sydney) is a Pluridisciplinary Designer dedicated to bold imagery and atmospheric exploration. With a background in Illustration, Product Design, and a BA Degree in Graphic Design at the Royal Academy of Art The Hague, her practice combines digital technology and precise craftsmanship to explore intuitive visual expression.
