Olivia Huynh (b.2004, Sydney, Australia) is an Pluridisciplinary Designer currently based in Paris, France. Her practice leans into bold imagery and atmospheric exploration. With a background in Illustration, Product Design, and a BA Degree in Graphic Design from the Royal Academy of Art The Hague, she combines emerging digital technology and precise craftsmanship to invent unique, innovative forms of visual expression.
