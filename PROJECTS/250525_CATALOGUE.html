Catalogue of Best Dutch Design Books

A catalogue showcasing five books nominated as 'Best Dutch Book Design' in 2025. The design of this publication aims to distill a personal experience into a visual framework, taking inspiration from my physcial movement through the library archive at Huis van het Boek along side the cognitive rhythm of my inner thoughts and observations while intuitively examining each book in all it's designed visual detail.

Publication
70 pages | 190 × 260 mm | English
The Hague, Netherlands · May 2025
